# Privacy notice

This page covers the visitors of docs.adriangoldner.dev. It is not about the data
the addons process; that is on [Privacy & retention](/guide/privacy). The
controller is Adrian Goldner, contact details are in the
[legal notice](https://adriangoldner.com/impressum). The general privacy policy
is at [adriangoldner.com/datenschutz](https://adriangoldner.com/datenschutz) (German).

## Reach measurement (Rybbit)

To count how the documentation is used we run **Rybbit**, a cookie-free analytics
tool that we host ourselves (server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany). No cookies are
set and nothing is stored on or read from your device. The script is delivered from
tr.adriangoldner.com, a domain we operate ourselves. Nothing is passed on to third
parties and visitors are not tracked across sites.

Only aggregated data that does not directly identify you is recorded: the page
viewed, the referring site, the approximate region, and the device and browser type.
IP addresses are not stored.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, a legitimate interest in data-minimising reach
measurement. Because Rybbit works without cookies and without access to your device,
no consent under section 25 TDDDG is required as things stand. You can object to the
processing at any time by email to info@adriangoldner.com.

## Hosting

The site is served from a server of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany, behind the
Cloudflare network, which sees your IP address when it delivers the pages.
